India has an interesting history of place names. Some names are as straightforward as the places they describe; called by the name of a river, a landscape, or a location. Others have stories that run deeper, shaped by languages, legends, rulers, and the layers of history that came before them. By breaking down the prefixes, suffixes, and roots hiding in these names, we can start to see these stories behind the map.

The other half — Prefixes

A lot of city names also rely on prefixes.

While the suffix can tell us what kind of place we are looking at, the prefix often gives us a clue about what made it distinctive. A river. A hill. An animal. A person. A landscape. These names commonly draw from a few recurring themes: founders, tribes and communities, geographical and water features, local flora and fauna, soil characteristics, religious deities, and caste or social groups. Together, they reveal what people considered distinctive or significant about a place.
