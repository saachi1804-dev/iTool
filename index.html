There's an old Indian saying: "Kos-kos par badle paani, chaar kos par vaani"—the water changes every few miles, and so does the language.While the saying celebrates India's rich linguistic diversity, it also raises a simple everyday question: How easily can we communicate as we travel across the country?

Languages rarely stop where political boundaries begin.
Some stretch across state borders, while others remain deeply rooted in a single region.

Using India's Census data on mother tongues, bilingualism, and trilingualism, the article estimates how easy would it be for you to communicate if you decide to travel within the country.
